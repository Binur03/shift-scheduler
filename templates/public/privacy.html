This policy explains what information {{ b.name }} ("we") keeps about the workers who use our shift-scheduling system, why, and who can see it.

Information we collect

  • Your name and mobile phone number
  • The shifts you are offered, accept, or decline
  • The times you check in and out of shifts, and whether that was by text or by the check-in page
  • Text messages you send to our scheduling number (for example IN, OUT, STOP)
  • Your check-in PIN, stored only in an irreversibly scrambled (hashed) form

How we use it

Only to run our work schedule: offering shifts, sending shift reminders and check-in links, recording attendance, and preparing payroll. We do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or text-message opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for any purpose other than running this work-notification program.

We use service providers only to operate this system: Twilio to deliver text messages and Google Cloud to host the application and database. They process information on our behalf and not for their own marketing. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and security

We keep attendance and payroll records for as long as needed for payroll and legal record-keeping. Access to the system is limited to authorized staff, connections are encrypted, and data is stored in access-controlled cloud infrastructure.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any of our texts to stop receiving them. To ask what information we hold about you, or to correct it, contact us below.
