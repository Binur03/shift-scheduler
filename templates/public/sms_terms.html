What this program is

{{ b.program }} is a work-notification text program run by {{ b.name }} for its own workers. Texts{% if b.sms_number %} come from {{ b.sms_number }} and{% endif %} cover: offers of open shifts with a link to accept or decline, a reminder before a confirmed shift with a check-in link, and confirmations when you text IN or OUT to record your arrival and departure. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.

How you opt in

Consent is collected verbally and in person. When you join our crew, before any text is sent, your supervisor reads you the script below and records your name, mobile number, the date, and that you agreed. We only text numbers that workers have given us for this purpose. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of any purchase, and workers who decline receive their schedule directly from their supervisor.

“Before I add you to the schedule: {{ b.name }} sends work-schedule text messages - shift offers, shift reminders and check-in links. Message frequency varies with your schedule. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP anytime to stop the texts, or HELP for help. Your mobile number and your consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Our SMS terms are at {{ url_for('public.sms_terms', _external=True) }} and our privacy policy is at {{ url_for('public.privacy', _external=True) }}. Do you agree to receive these text messages at this number? If you agree, I am adding you now and you will get a text from this number.”

This is the script our supervisors read, word for word. It is the same script registered with our messaging provider.

How to opt out or get help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You'll get one confirmation and no further texts.
  • Reply START to receive texts again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.

If you opt out, you can still get your schedule directly from your supervisor.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with the work schedule. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or text-message opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for any purpose other than running this work-notification program. Mobile information is used only to send you the work messages described above. See our Privacy Policy.
